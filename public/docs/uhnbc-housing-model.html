Post-Construction Opportunity: Healthcare Staff Housing

When construction wraps in 2032, the 600 units don't go idle — the UHNBC acute care expansion will require significant new clinical staffing (estimated 200–400 new healthcare positions). ERS transitions the same housing stock from construction workforce to healthcare workforce under a long-term institutional partnership with Northern Health. This positions ERS to layer directly into the NH LIFE Model (10-year master lease framework) with zero gap in occupancy.
