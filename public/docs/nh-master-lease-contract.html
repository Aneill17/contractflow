Service Architecture
Three Pillars · What ERS Provides · How It's Priced

Northern Health pays one invoice per month. That invoice covers three service pillars — housing rental, property management, and support services. All priced and modelled below.

🏠
Pillar A — Housing & Rental
  • Fully furnished, serviced units adjacent to hospital sites
  • ERS sources, leases, and furnishes every unit
  • NH staff check in — ERS handles everything else
  • Flexible occupancy: short-stay locum or long-term rotating staff
  • Linen, WiFi, utilities included
Rate Per Unit
$3,150/mo
$105/night × 30 days · standard ERS rate
⚙️
Pillar B — Property Management
  • Full landlord relationship management
  • Maintenance coordination and rapid response
  • Unit sourcing and onboarding as NH needs grow
  • Occupancy tracking and reporting dashboard
  • NH never manages a landlord, a lease, or a repair
Management Fee
$275/unit/mo
~8.7% of rental value · industry standard 8–12%
🧹
Pillar C — Support Services
  • Regular cleaning & linen service
  • Turnover cleaning between staff rotations
  • Admin & consulting retainer — program management, NH liaison, onboarding coordination, compliance reporting
  • 24/7 support line for NH staff
Support Services
$175/unit/mo + retainer
Cleaning $175/unit + admin retainer (tiered by scale)
Single Invoice: Northern Health receives one monthly invoice covering all three pillars. The line items are transparent and auditable — Housing, Property Management, and Support Services — but the delivery is seamless. No internal admin required from NH.
