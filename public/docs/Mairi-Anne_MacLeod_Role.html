Section 01
Overview
Logistics Coordinator

The Mandate

Mairi-Anne is the operational backbone of ERS housing — the person who makes sure every unit is ready before a tenant arrives, every maintenance request gets resolved, and every move happens without friction. She bridges the gap between the properties and the people who live in them.

Why This Role Exists

As ERS scales its housing portfolio, the day-to-day logistics of unit readiness, tenant arrivals, cleaners, and maintenance can't stay on Austin's plate. Mairi-Anne owns the physical side of operations — so the team can focus on growth without operational drag.

The Role in Context

This is a part-time, contractual, as-needed role within Elias Range Inc. Mairi-Anne works closely with Laurie McPherson (Director of Operations), Megan Banks (HR & Leasing), and Angela Hayward (Guest Services Lead). She is the person on the ground — coordinating the physical environment so the rest of the team can deliver a consistent resident experience.

Section 02 — Schedule A
Responsibilities
1
Unit Logistics & Onboarding
  • Coordinate unit setup and readiness for incoming staff (furniture check, supplies, keys, access)
  • Manage move-in and move-out logistics for all housing units
  • Conduct and document unit inspections (move-in/move-out condition reports)
  • Coordinate cleaning between tenants — schedule cleaners, confirm completion
  • Maintain unit inventory lists and flag replacement/repair needs
2
Guest & Staff Coordination
  • Serve as first point of contact for new tenant arrivals
  • Distribute welcome packages, keys, parking passes, and house manuals
  • Coordinate on-site logistics for deployment assignments
  • Support Angela Hayward (Guest Services Lead) with tenant communication as needed
3
Vendor & Maintenance Coordination
  • Manage relationships with cleaners, maintenance contractors, and suppliers
  • Obtain quotes for repairs and coordinate scheduling with landlords/owners
  • Track open maintenance requests and follow up to completion
  • Document all vendor interactions and invoices
4
Administrative Support
  • Maintain unit status tracking (occupied/vacant/cleaning/maintenance) in ContractFlow
  • Update occupant records, lease dates, and unit notes in the system
  • Support Megan Banks (HR & Leasing) with unit-related documentation
  • Assist with contract setup logistics for new housing units being onboarded
  • Prepare and submit expense reports for reimbursements
5
Deployment & On-Site Support
  • Available for deployment assignments at client sites (construction/healthcare locations)
  • Conduct site visits to assess unit conditions and client needs
  • Report site observations and issues to Director of Operations
